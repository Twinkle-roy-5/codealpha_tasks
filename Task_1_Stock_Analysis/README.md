# Stock Analysis Report

## Project Overview
This project analyses three major Indian listed companies: Reliance Industries, Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), and HDFC Bank. The objective is to compare their historical stock price performance, valuation metrics, market capitalisation, and recent company developments.

## Companies Analysed
- **Reliance Industries** — Energy, retail, and digital services
- **Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)** — Information technology services
- **HDFC Bank** — Banking and financial services

## Analysis Performed
- Historical stock price trend analysis
- One-year adjusted closing price return calculation
- Price-to-Earnings (P/E) ratio comparison
- Market capitalisation comparison
- Review of recent company news and developments
- Comparative findings and conclusion

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Historical stock market data
- Financial websites and company announcements
- Microsoft Word / PDF for report preparation

## Key Findings
The analysis compares the one-year adjusted price returns of the three companies and examines differences in valuation and market capitalisation. It also considers company-specific developments that may influence investor sentiment.

The results are historical observations and do not predict future stock performance.
